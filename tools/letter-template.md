# Personal letter template

A plain-text starting point for writing the list directly, as yourself, when
you have something to say that isn't tied to a new post. (The automated post
emails are handled by `newsletter.py`; this is the other thing.)

Copy the block between the lines into a new Buttondown email, or keep it as a
Buttondown saved template. Plain text only. No images.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Subject: 

Hi all,

[Whatever you want to say. One idea per paragraph. Short is good; a few
lines is plenty.]

[Pointing people somewhere? Put the link on its own line so it's easy to tap:]
https://theodore.net/

Teddy
----------------------------------------------------------------------

## Notes

- Subject: short and plain, the same as the post emails. No prefix, no emoji.
- Replies land in teddy@warner.net, so "just reply" actually reaches you.
- Buttondown adds the unsubscribe line automatically. Don't add your own footer.
- Keep the voice yours. No em dashes, no filler.
- Preview before sending: save as a draft, then use Buttondown's "Send a test
  email" to test@theodore.net and read it on your phone first.
